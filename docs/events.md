Emitted as of M0 (server): user.registered · user.role_granted (identity-kyc, via outbox).
Emitted as of M1 (server): config.micro_market_changed (analytics-config) · merchant.created · merchant.submitted · merchant.approved · merchant.rejected · merchant.went_live · merchant.staff_added (merchant-catalogue) · merchant.kyc_updated (identity-kyc KycService). Client (merchant app, M1): onboarding_step_completed(step) · kyc_result(kind, status) — sent to PostHog once the SDK is wired (see ADR-006).
Emitted as of M2 (server): merchant.updated · merchant.catalogue_confirmed · merchant.catalogue_went_stale · merchant.slots_updated · item.created · item.updated · item.price_changed · item.stock_changed · item.deleted · item.approved · item.released · item.rejected (merchant-catalogue) · media.uploaded · media.approved · media.rejected (media pipeline). Client (merchant app, M2): barcode_scanned(result: master|open_barcode|unknown, already_listed) · catalogue_item_created(source: barcode|unknown_barcode|manual, status) · catalogue_item_updated · catalogue_stock_changed(stock, scope: item|size) · catalogue_confirmed(items) · catalogue_assisted_approved(items, held) · catalogue_imported(created, rejected) · onboarding_step_completed(step: photos).

Client (consumer app, M3): app_open(app, version) · location_set(source: gps|pin|address) · serviceability_result(serviceable, micro_market, waitlist) · feed_viewed(tab, store_count, micro_market, from_cache) · search_performed(query, category, results, store_groups, zero_result, latency_ms) · merchant_viewed(merchant_id, type, delivers_here, from_cache) · item_viewed(item_id, merchant_id, variant_count, price_paise) · add_to_cart(merchant_id, item_id, variant_id, price_paise, source: search|store|sheet|cart, cart_count) · cart_merchant_switch(from_merchant_id, to_merchant_id) · slot_selected(merchant_id, slot_id, date, start). Emitted as of M4 (server): order.created (online order waiting for payment) · order.placed · order.payment_failed (orders) · payment.intent_created · payment.captured · payment.failed · wallet.credited (payments-settlement). cart-pricing consumes order.placed (confirm promo, clear cart) and order.payment_failed / cancelled / rejected / expired (release promo budget); orders consumes payment.captured; payments consumes order.payment_failed. Promo create/edit/pause/resume write audit rows.

Emitted as of M5 (server): order.accepted · order.preparing · order.rejected · order.expired · order.cancelled · order.ready · order.item_removed · order.item_substituted (orders, via the guarded transition and outbox) · merchant.availability_changed · merchant.auto_paused (merchant-catalogue) · wallet.credited (refunds for cancellations and item changes). notifications consumes order.* and merchant.auto_paused (push/SMS/WhatsApp/voice, logged per order); the realtime gateway fans out order.new / order.updated / store.paused; merchants consumes order.expired / accepted / rejected for auto-pause; the search indexer consumes availability changes. Timer jobs (not events): order.accept_timeout · order.remind_merchant_sms · order.remind_merchant_call.
Client (merchant app, M5): order_accepted(order_id) · order_rejected(order_id, reason) · order_marked_ready(order_id) · order_item_unavailable(order_id, action: remove|substitute) · store_availability_changed(open).
Client (consumer app, M5): order_cancelled(order_id, reason) · whatsapp_opt_in_changed(on).

Emitted as of M6 (server): order.picked_up · order.out_for_delivery · order.delivered (orders, via the guarded transition and outbox; actor rider or staff) · order.cancelled with reason no_courier (customer cancel after the fallback ladder). dispatch-tracking consumes order.accepted (fulfilment_mode, staff_id), order.ready and order.cancelled. Notifications: rider.new_offer · staff.delivery_link · merchant.rider_assigned · merchant.self_delivery_bonus · customer.out_for_delivery · customer.order_delivered · customer.delivery_delayed · customer.finding_courier. fulfilment.* outbox events arrive with settlement in M7 (ADR-012). Client (rider app, M6): online_toggled(online, zones) · offer_accepted(offer_id, pay_paise) · offer_declined(offer_id) · picked_up(job_id) · delivered(job_id, verification, cod_paise).
Client (merchant app, M6): fulfilment_choice(order_id, mode, staff, after_fallback).
Client (consumer app, M6): order_cancelled(order_id, reason: no_courier).

Emitted as of M7 (server): wallet.credited (refunds to the wallet) · order refunds recorded on the order (refundedPaise). Settlement runs, payout transfers, refund approvals / rejections, reconciliation runs / resolutions and dispute decisions write audit rows (settlement.run, payout.retried, refund.first_approval, refund.approved, refund.rejected, recon.run, recon.resolved, dispute.accepted, dispute.rejected). refund.issued / issue.resolved / settlement.run_completed outbox events arrive with the analytics pipeline in M8 (ADR-013).
Client (consumer app, M7): issue_reported(order_id, type) · rating_submitted(order_id, store_stars, rider_stars).
Client (merchant app, M7): dispute_raised(refund_id).

Emitted as of M8 (server): the analytics export forwards the domain events in `EXPORTED_EVENTS` (`growth.service.ts`) to PostHog, with ids, states and amounts only: user.registered, merchant.went_live, merchant.auto_paused, order.placed, order.accepted, order.rejected, order.expired, order.ready, order.picked_up, order.delivered, order.cancelled, payment.captured, payment.failed and wallet.credited. Ops actions write audit rows: grievance.acknowledged, grievance.resolved, data_request.completed (for an erasure, the account is anonymised in the same step), data_request.rejected, review.published, review.hidden and lead.updated. The business reports (`/admin/reports`) are computed from the tables. refund.issued, issue.resolved and settlement.run_completed stay deferred until a warehouse consumes them (ADR-014).
Client (consumer app, M8): consent_changed(purpose, granted) · data_export_requested · account_deletion_requested · grievance_raised(category).
Client (merchant app, M8): review_replied(order_id).

Auth, WhatsApp OTP (ADR-017): no new analytics events. `POST /v1/auth/otp/request` and `/v1/auth/ops/login` responses carry `channel` (whatsapp|sms); API logs "OTP sent" (channel, fallback) and "OTP channel failed" (channel, metaCode). Feature flag: `whatsapp_otp`.

Ratings & reviews (ADR-018), server: **rating.submitted**(order_id, merchant_id, rider_user_id, store_stars, rider_stars, has_comment, flagged) when a customer rates a delivered order, and **rating.moderated**(order_id, merchant_id, from, to) when ops hides or publishes a review. Both are written in the same transaction as the rating (outbox; in-process in fake mode). The search indexer consumes both and rebuilds the store, so store cards, ranking and `minRating` use the current rating (hidden reviews excluded).
Client (consumer app, ratings): rating_submitted gains has_comment · feed_viewed gains top_rated (the "4★ & above" chip).

Beyond the radius and Deliver anywhere (ADR-022), server: merchant.updated with fields [deliversAnywhere]; order bills can carry a beyond_radius fee line. Client (merchant app): delivers_anywhere_changed(on).

City-wide stores and the Open switch (ADR-021), server: merchant.availability_changed now carries open_until (ISO time or null) · merchant.updated with fields [hours] when ops set a store's hours. Audit action: merchant.hours_changed. Client (merchant app): store_hours_updated(closed_days). Client (consumer app): merchant_viewed.delivers_here already reports stores opened out of range.

Service coverage (ADR-019), server: **config.city_coverage_changed**(city_id, before, after) · merchant.updated with fields [radiusM] when ops change a store's radius. Audit actions: city.coverage_changed, merchant.radius_changed.
Client (consumer app, ADR-019): gps_fix(accuracy_m, precise) when "Use current location" gets a fix; location_set(source: gps) once the customer confirms it on the pin.
